Open SEO Crawler

A free Screaming Frog alternative you run on your own computer. Point it at a site and it walks every page looking for technical SEO problems. No URL cap, no account, no cloud.

It also checks what AI search sees. After a crawl it requests a sample of pages as Googlebot, Bingbot, GPTBot, PerplexityBot and friends, then flags any page a crawler gets blocked from, redirected away from, or handed a thinner version of.

  • One-line install on Linux, macOS or Windows, updates itself daily

SEO Side Panel

A Chrome extension that stays open beside every page you visit and audits it from the page's own code. Every finding comes with the fix written next to it.

It answers two questions most SEO extensions skip: what Google actually gets from this URL, and what ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity get, which is usually not the same thing.

  • Titles and descriptions measured in pixels, not characters
  • Raw HTML next to the rendered page, so you see what AI crawlers miss
  • JSON-LD errors named by block and line
  • No account, no server, no AI

Bulk AI CLI Connector

A free plugin on WordPress.org that lets Claude, ChatGPT, MCP agents and your own scripts read and rewrite a site's SEO in bulk over the REST API. There is no AI inside it and it makes no requests of its own. Each call signs in as a WordPress user and can only do what that user could do in wp-admin.

WORLD 1-4 · SELF-HOSTED

robots.txt Tester

Google retired its robots.txt Tester in 2023. This brings it back with proper RFC 9309 matching, and adds the bit the original never had: one click tests a URL against every well-known search and AI crawler.

Most free checkers use Python's built-in parser, which ignores * and $ wildcards and happily says "allowed" when Googlebot would be blocked. This one follows Google's real rules: the most specific group wins, the longest pattern wins, ties go to Allow.
